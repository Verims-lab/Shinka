# 078 — Centered visible bottom navigation

Buildmarker-078 / HC-077.0. Clean AydanTest fast-forwarded from ebcd1da to origin/main 6fcc651 before editing. The trees were identical. No main write, PR, Base44 publication or live-data operation belongs to this package.

## Cause and correction

Measurements on the actual local App with isolated SDK transport reproduced the issue on Home, Calendar and Pattern. At 390px width, the unchanged icon/label group centers 23px below the top of the nav, including its 1px border. The 44px control row was already centered. The nav also owned the system bottom padding, extending the painted rectangle to 69px at a 24px inset and 79px at a 34px inset. No extra ancestor/footer margin or shell padding was found.

ProductNavigation now has one transparent outer shinka-nav-safe-area that owns the existing safe-bottom padding and flex-shrink rule. The inner painted nav contains the same border and row. Its normal-text visible height is 45px at all insets; total occupied footer remains 45px plus the system inset. Controls do not move into the home-indicator area. This changes the painted area's boundary, without increasing height or adding arbitrary positional offsets. At zero inset the appearance is unchanged. Enlarged text can grow the row.

The existing links, routes, active state, reselect behavior, focus indicators, 20px icons, caption styling, colors and equal thirds are preserved. Home/Calendar/Pattern share the same component; their content layout remains unchanged. The original global PNG and manual theme behavior are retained. This supersedes only the historical 057/059 requirement that the painted nav itself owns the system inset.
